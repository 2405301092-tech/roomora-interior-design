Our story

Design Belongs to Everyone

ROOMORA is a student-created interior design website concept that helps homeowners, renters, students and design lovers explore inspiring spaces and personalize design ideas.

Our mission is simple: make good design feel welcoming, accessible and expressive. From color palettes to room inspiration, every choice should tell your story.
